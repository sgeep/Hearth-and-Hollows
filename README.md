Hearth & Hollows is a small top-down fantasy life sim/tavern management/action roguelite game I'm building mostly through vibe coding with Claude.

Idea is inspired by Stardew Valley, Dave the Diver, and Delicious in Dungeon: run and customize your own tavern, spend your days around a small fantasy village farming, fishing, ranching, and getting to know its residents, then open the tavern in the evening and delve into the Hollows, the strange underworld beneath the village, at night for rare ingredients, loot, quests, and strange things to bring home.

I've tinkered with Unity before, but never made it much further than basic prototypes. This project is partly about making something I'd enjoy playing, and partly about learning game design and development through dialogue, NPC relationships, meaningful customization, immersive minigames, and top-down combat.

I'm leaning on a few Unity Asset Store tools I've picked up along the way, including TopDown Engine, Feel, Super Text Mesh, Dialogue System, and Quest Machine. Most of the game's art currently comes from the Minifantasy collection by Krishna Palacio.

Currently in development using Unity.
