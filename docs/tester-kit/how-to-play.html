Hearth & Hollows

You've inherited Tally Ho!, the tavern in the odd little village of Kariaston, from someone who vanished into the Hollows beneath it. By day you live in the village; in the evening you cook and serve; at night you go down below for what the surface can't grow.

Build being tested: 0.4i-d.393 (the menu's bottom-right corner shows yours)

What we're asking

Play at least two in-game days. A day ends when you go to sleep. An hour is plenty.

Then fill in the questionnaire from your invitation. It takes about five minutes.

There are no wrong answers. If something confused you, that's the game's fault, not yours, and it's exactly what we want to hear about.

Starting the game

In the browser

Open the itch page, enter the password from your invitation, and press Run game. Use Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer (phones and tablets aren't supported). The first load takes a few seconds; click once inside the game so its sound can start. The fullscreen button is under the game, at the right.

On Windows

Download the Windows zip from the same page, unzip it anywhere, and run HearthAndHollows.exe. Windows may say it doesn't recognise the publisher: choose More info, then Run anyway. (The build isn't signed yet.)

How a day works

  • MorningYou wake upstairs in your room. Go down, talk to Orik (the dwarf at the bar) and Boog (the goblin cook), and step outside.
  • DaytimeExplore Kariaston, meet the villagers, plant and tend the garden beds, and buy ingredients at Musashi's market cart. Planting and tending use Vigor; walking and talking never do. Time only runs while you're out and about.
  • EveningWhen you're ready, use the menu board in the tavern to start the evening. Choose tonight's dishes, open the doors, cook at the stations and carry plates to the customers.
  • NightAfter the evening you go down the hatch into the Hollows. Fight, harvest monster parts for tomorrow's cooking, and climb the rope out before your Essence (the blue bar) runs out.
  • SleepBack home, go to sleep. The game saves, and a new day begins.

A few tips

  • The pause menu (Esc or Start) has the controls for every part of the game, the options, and quit.
  • Your first day is a little different: the story starts with an arrival and a first trip below.
  • In the Hollows, a clean kill gives better ingredients than smashing a monster to bits. A low, just-hit enemy can sometimes be finished off with F or LT for the best parts.
  • If you die in the Hollows you keep one slot of what you carried (you choose which), and nothing you've already brought home.
  • Tab (or View on a controller) inside the tavern lets you move the furniture around.

Known issues

  • A few sounds are stand-ins: the grill burning, the low-Essence heartbeat, bumping into someone carrying a plate, the tap overflowing warning, and decorating's sounds. Tell us if any of them grate.
  • No controller rumble in the browser. Browsers can't drive it; the Windows build does.
  • Quitting in the middle of a delve loses that delve (the game asks first). Continue starts the night's delve again from the top. This is on purpose.
  • Customers can walk through each other and through you, and they sit with their standing pose.
  • Enemies' hits don't knock you back.
  • The main menu has no music, on purpose. The first music you hear is in the Hollows.

Starting over

The easy way: on the main menu, choose New Game. It asks before replacing your game.

Clearing everything, options too:

  • Browser: remove the game's stored data for itch. In Chrome or Edge open chrome://settings/content/all (Edge: edge://settings/content/all), search for itch.zone and delete its data. In Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Manage Data, then remove itch.zone.
  • Windows: close the game and delete the folder %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\sagaphy\Hearthdelve (paste that into the File Explorer address bar to open it).

If something goes wrong

Note what you were doing just before, and put it in the questionnaire's "Did anything break?" answer. On Windows, the file Player.log in the folder above helps a lot if the game crashed: attach it if you can.
