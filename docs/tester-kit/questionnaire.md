# Hearth & Hollows: playtest questionnaire

Ready to paste into Google Forms or Tally, one question per item, in this order. The type to choose is in brackets; "required" means the form should require an answer. About five minutes for a tester.

**Form description:** Thank you for playing! Please answer after you've stopped (or whenever you get stuck). There are no wrong answers: if something confused you, that's the game's fault, not yours.

---

## About your game

1. **Which version did you play?** It's in the bottom-right corner of the main menu and the pause menu, for example `0.4i-d.391+1a2b3c4d`. [short answer, required]
2. **How did you play?** [multiple choice, required]
   - In the browser on the itch page
   - The Windows download
   - Both
3. **Which browser?** (Only if you played in the browser.) [multiple choice]
   - Chrome
   - Edge
   - Firefox
   - Safari
   - Other
4. **What did you play with?** [multiple choice, required]
   - Keyboard and mouse
   - A controller
   - Both, switching between them
5. **Which controller?** (Only if you used one.) [multiple choice]
   - Xbox
   - PlayStation
   - Nintendo Switch Pro
   - Other (please say which)
6. **Roughly how long did you play?** [multiple choice, required]
   - Under 20 minutes
   - 20 to 45 minutes
   - 45 to 90 minutes
   - Longer
7. **How many in-game days did you finish?** (A day ends when you sleep.) [multiple choice, required]
   - None yet
   - 1
   - 2
   - 3 or 4
   - 5 or more

## Finding your way

8. **Was there a moment you didn't know what to do next?** What were you trying to do, and what did you try? [paragraph, required]
9. **How clear was the game about what to do?** [linear scale 1–5, 1 "lost most of the time", 5 "always knew"]
10. **Was the text easy to read?** [linear scale 1–5, 1 "hard to read", 5 "easy"]

## The parts of the game

## Problems

16. **Did anything break?** (Something stuck, a crash, a frozen screen, a character in the wrong place.) What happened, and what were you doing just before? [paragraph]
17. **Did anything look or sound wrong?** [paragraph]
18. **How smoothly did it run?** [multiple choice]
    - Smooth
    - The odd stutter
    - Often choppy
    - Too slow to play
19. **Your computer, if you know it** (for example "a five-year-old laptop" or "a gaming PC"). [short answer]
